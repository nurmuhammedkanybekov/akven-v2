\section*{Use of generative AI tools}
During this project I used generative AI assistants as development and writing aids: to draft and review parts of the
source code and the tests, to check my English, and to help structure parts of this document. I defined the problem,
the requirements and the design decisions, reviewed and tested all generated material, and I take full responsibility
for the content of the software and of this thesis. The language model used inside the product as a negotiation
assistant (Section~\ref{sec:assistant}) is a separate component of the system and is evaluated in
Chapter~\ref{ch:test}.
